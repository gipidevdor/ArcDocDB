## Correctness

The isolated build passes 240 tests plus two smoke checks, without warnings/style-warnings.
The 20 CSN tests compare the product with independent Lisp integers and lists:

- K=2, six commits: 850 prefixes and 243 complete histories;
- K=3, six commits: 7,310 prefixes and 2,403 complete histories;
- 10,000 actions, seed `#x46c5a71b`: assignment, full rejection, resolution,
  reading, and stale tokens; carry at 2^32, fixnum boundary, maximum u64;
- four workers, 1,000 assignments each, K=3: unique contiguous identities,
  reuse of every slot, busy/full rejections without consuming CSNs, monotonic and convergent H;
- mutex held by another worker, recursive acquisition, and macro with multiple
  values, NIL, error, and nonlocal exit: no waiting in product, and cleanup;
- shape, frontier, and cardinality FI: typed errors before mutation.

All four mutants are detected by named probes after successful compilation:
omitted registration, premature H, omitted high carry, accepted stale identity.
Probes are dedicated and separate from the ASDF suite. Source copies,
drivers, probes, and raw logs are preserved; compilation error does not count as detection.

`sb-cover`: `registry.lisp` 361/387 expressions (93.3%) and 68/72 branches (94.4%).
The four untraversed directions belong to type checks generated by
`defstruct`, on capacity and vector domains. Declarations and load
forms contribute to the expression denominator. Raw state and HTML
are preserved; no MC/DC coverage or exhaustive-interleaving claim.

<a id="allocazioni-e-costo-sequenziale"></a>

## Allocations and sequential cost

60 final samples: 50 assignment/resolution and 10 consistent frontier
reads. All windows have positive duration, at least 20 ticks, and **zero bytes
of measured heap**. The positive control sees 16 MiB of deliberate allocations;
the constant baseline stays zero. The two bases are `(0,0)` and
`(#x80000000,#xfffffff0)`, the second beyond fixnum and across low carry.

Medians of five samples; a pair includes two APIs, not two documents:

| Scenario | Small base, millions/s | High base, millions/s |
|---|---:|---:|
| Assign/resolve, K=1 | 5.25 | 5.34 |
| Assign/resolve, K=16 | 4.53 | 4.39 |
| Assign/resolve, K=256 | 1.46 | 1.44 |
| Assign/resolve, K=1024 | 0.461 | 0.461 |
| Oldest pending, second slot reused, K=2 | 4.89 | 4.85 |
| Consistent read of last and H | 13.29 | 13.30 |

The K-word scan dominates as K grows; default 256 remains an
initial configuration to reassess in the WAL path. No heap or cost of the
controller, index, document, or flush is inferred from these measurements.

<a id="worker-reali"></a>

## Real workers

Three replicates per mode, 10,000 pairs per worker, K=256, fixtures and threads
created before the window. Median pairs/s:

| Workers | One shared Archivio, millions/s | One Archivio per worker, millions/s |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1.29 | 1.26 |
| 2 | 1.16 | 2.47 |
| 4 | 1.01 | 5.14 |

Every replicate checks exact emitted identities with bitmap and checksum,
and last=H at completion. In the shared case, three replicates total 50,644
busy retries with two workers and 348,290 with four; full is zero. In independent
registries, busy/full are zero. Sharing per Archivio serializes coordination
**per batch**, as the ADR requires; per-document scalability is not inferred.
Contention conditions allocate. Worker allocation windows use
a process counter, overlap, and cannot be summed.

<a id="diagnostiche-e-integrazione"></a>

## Diagnostics and integration

The first campaign retained timings, work, and heap but lost derived
throughput fields: `getf` on a new key prepended an unshared head.
The recorder now preallocates both metric fields, verifies their
persistence in records, and includes a C4 check. All three campaigns,
failed reading attempts, and sources of both diagnostic helpers are preserved.
These errors were in collection tools; no CSN product failure
was observed in the described campaigns.

Manifest and CBOR headers added meanwhile on `main` are integrated before the
full delivery check. The catalog preserves the actual outcome of
`make check`; isolated tests do not replace it.
WAL/controller, recovery global maximum, snapshots, parking, and fail-stop
remain subsequent integrations. No complete requirement is promoted.

<a id="addendum-sulla-cache-dello-strumento-di-copertura--2026-10-09"></a>

## Addendum on the coverage tool cache — 2026-10-09

The [WAL–CSN campaign](wal-csn-risultati.md) found that the previous
ASDF mapping of only the root did not enforce a private cache for descendant
files. The tool was corrected with recursive wildcards and a
destination check before compilation. CSN reports above remain
observations from their campaign; isolation claimed by the old
configuration is not retroactively certified. No historical data is
replaced by results from the subsequent campaign.
