This is a repository and distribution review, not a legal opinion, ownership
certification, trademark/patent clearance or commercial release approval. The
inspected base is `b18c02f6eed9a2130383b350feff3ad97b3d67c8`. Source archives,
licenses, site code and primary legal sources were inspected. Initial failed
checks are retained with the follow-up evidence.

The maintainer confirmed a **personal project published as a private individual**,
Giacomo Picchiarelli, and requested **GitHub contact**, without adding a personal
contact address. This records the stated model, not every jurisdiction's legal
classification. Public issues can request a confidential route without including
the substance of a request; they are not a private legal inbox. The adequacy of
that route must be assessed for personal-data processing the operator controls.

## Upstream inspection

The 61 archives pinned in `tools/pages/dependencies.lock.lisp` were rehashed.
Original license files, embedded source notices or upstream declarations were
collected. Terms such as `LLGPL` are retained as upstream wording rather than
translated into a guessed SPDX identifier. This review does not redistribute
the cached toolchain as project code.

The browser review covers 71 entries (72 notice records) in
`tools/pages/third-party-notices.lisp`, including the source-map-only POSIX path
implementation. The first strict substring check flagged two TypeFox notices:
their source files use CRLF, and the collection omits edge blank lines. Follow-up
comparison permits only line-ending and edge-newline normalization. These are
not missing license clauses and must not be described as byte-for-byte copies.
The first failed report and all original hashes are retained. No Node.js, npm or
npx was used or installed during this review.

LGPL/LLGPL findings concern **documentation generation**, not a new runtime
dependency. Using a tool does not by itself show that its implementation was
copied into output. Protected templates or implementations actually incorporated
in output would need separate analysis. The custom CSS/script and explicit
Mermaid bundle define the inspected browser delivery boundary.

## Open factual and legal matters

Keep GitHub as the requested contact. Do not invent an email, postal address,
corporate identity or certification. Before commercial distribution or operating
a personal-data service, establish the responsible operator, workable private
rights-request handling, actual bundled components and applicable obligations
with competent legal advice. Obtain contributor/employer and artwork provenance
records where needed. No technical checker closes those matters by itself.
